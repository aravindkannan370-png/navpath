## Long Description

Start your journey toward a maritime career with NavPath Academy — a modern online learning platform designed for students preparing for IMU CET, DNS and Merchant Navy career opportunities.

Whether you are preparing for IMU CET 2027, appearing for the first time, or preparing again as an IMU CET repeater, NavPath Academy provides structured learning resources to help you prepare with greater confidence.

### IMU CET Preparation

Prepare for IMU CET with structured courses covering important subjects and concepts. Learn at your own pace through video lessons, study materials and practice resources.

### IMU CET 2027

Build a consistent preparation routine with organized courses, lessons and assessments designed to support your IMU CET 2027 preparation.

### Mock Tests & Practice

Test your knowledge with mock tests and assessments. Practice regularly, understand your performance and identify areas that need more attention.

### Study Materials

Access organized study materials alongside your courses to support revision and strengthen your understanding of important topics.

### DNS Preparation

Students interested in the DNS pathway can use NavPath Academy for structured preparation and learning resources while building their knowledge and confidence.

### Merchant Navy Preparation

NavPath Academy goes beyond entrance-exam preparation. Explore learning resources and career guidance designed for students who want to build a future in the Merchant Navy.

### Track Your Progress

Keep track of your learning journey through your student dashboard. View your courses, lessons and progress so you can stay organized and focused.

### Learn Online

Access your learning experience through a modern online platform designed for convenient study across desktop and mobile devices.

### Designed for Students

NavPath Academy is designed for:
