# NavPath Academy — ASO Proposal

## 1. ASO Objective

NavPath Academy will target students and parents searching for
IMU CET preparation, Merchant Navy preparation and DNS preparation.

The ASO strategy will focus on search relevance, user intent,
conversion and long-term discoverability rather than keyword stuffing.

## 3. Keyword Prioritization

### IMU CET

"IMU CET" is the core search term because it directly represents
the primary examination supported by NavPath Academy.

### IMU CET 2027

"IMU CET 2027" targets students actively preparing for the upcoming
admission cycle and represents a high-intent search.

### IMU CET Preparation

This keyword targets students who have already decided to prepare
for the examination and are looking for learning resources.

### Merchant Navy

This keyword captures users who are interested in the broader
maritime career rather than only the entrance examination.

### DNS Coaching / DNS Preparation

These keywords target students considering the DNS pathway and
expand NavPath Academy's reach beyond IMU CET.

### IMU CET Mock Test

This targets users looking specifically for practice and
assessment resources.
## 4. Keyword Strategy

NavPath Academy will avoid excessive repetition of keywords.

Keywords will be incorporated naturally into:

- App name
- Short description
- Long description
- App screenshots
- Feature graphic
- Promotional content

The primary objective is to make the listing useful and
convincing to students and parents while maintaining natural
language.

The strategy will prioritize relevance and conversion over
keyword stuffing.

### MarinePrep — Competitor Analysis

MarinePrep is a direct competitor focused primarily on IMU-CET
preparation.

Current Google Play positioning includes structured study
materials, mock tests, notes, question banks, progress tracking,
AI-assisted learning and resume-learning functionality.

The app currently has 5K+ Google Play downloads and was updated
in July 2026.

#### Strengths

- Very clear IMU-CET positioning
- Strong focus on structured preparation
- Mock tests and question bank
- Progress tracking
- AI-assisted learning
- IMU-CET 2027 positioning
- Clear exam-focused messaging

#### NavPath Opportunity

NavPath Academy should avoid competing only on the number of
study features.

Instead, NavPath can position itself as a broader maritime
learning platform covering:

IMU CET → DNS → Merchant Navy → Career Preparation

The NavPath experience should combine exam preparation with
course learning, progress tracking, study materials, tests and
career guidance.

This gives NavPath a broader "maritime career journey"
position rather than only an IMU-CET preparation position.
